The prototype's missing flag and repair therefore contradicts what the paper says about MemState.
2. **Line 131 Framing, "it does not claim C3":** literally true of §4.3, but it leaves out P1:240 and the "(C3)" tags at P1:275 and P1:279. Reword as in the table so the Framing does not set up the wrong conclusion in item 1.
