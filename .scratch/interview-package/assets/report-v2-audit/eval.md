## Ruling on the "four of five backbones" qualifier
**The old wording is correct; the new draft overstates.** MemTX Table 12 (MemTX:492-493) gives rollback recall on Qwen2.5-14B (column 2) as **0.959 for MemTX and 0.963 for Cordon + revocation**. Both are 1.000 on the other four backbones. The text says the same thing: "only the two cascade carriers reach 1.0 on four backbones" (MemTX:470), and "rollback recall 1.0 on four backbones, 0.96 on the capability floor" (MemTX:200). The paper calls Qwen2.5-14B "a capability floor" (MemTX:216), so "weakest" is a fair gloss. The qualifier must be restored.
